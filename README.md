### Hi, I'm Fabian 👋

I'm an engineer who likes to understand systems all the way down.
I design boards in KiCad, write firmware for STM32 and ESP32, put neural nets on FPGAs,
and build forecasting models and the infrastructure to run them.
My favourite problems sit where hardware, data and software meet.

<sub>Currently co-founder at NexPatch AI · based in Leipzig/Erfurt, Germany</sub>

---

## 🔧 What I build

| Layer | What I do |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Hardware & Embedded** | PCB design (KiCad), STM32/ESP32 firmware, RF interfaces, laser drivers |
| 🧮 **FPGA & Digital** | VHDL, hardware implementations of neural networks |
| 📈 **Machine Learning** | Time series forecasting, transformer architectures, custom losses (PyTorch, TensorFlow) |
| 🖥️ **Systems & Infra** | Self-hosted LLM serving, Docker, Kubernetes, GPU servers |
| 🌐 **Web** | TypeScript, Next.js, Angular |
